## **Instruction: Background-Merged Imagery & Video**

When using images or video in the design, apply the following rules:

1. **No frames, no borders, no hard edges.**
   Images and videos must visually blend into the background, with edges dissolved, feathered, or softly diffused.

2. **Merged-texture appearance.**
   The imagery should feel like part of the background texture itself — not like a placed rectangle.
   Use techniques such as:

   * Soft gradient masks
   * Feathered edges
   * Subtle transparency
   * Noise blending
   * Overlay modes (multiply, screen, soft-light, etc.)

3. **Background-integrated video.**
   Videos should run as if embedded in the environment, appearing as a natural extension of the background surface.

   * No visible container
   * No corner radius
   * No shadow
   * Motion feels like it's occurring *behind* the content layers
   * Apply atmospheric blending (fog, tint, grain, or texture overlay)

4. **Dissolve effect.**
   Both images and videos must fade into the background through gradient fades or organic edge breakup.
   They should look like they "melt" or "evaporate" into the page environment.

5. **Maintain legibility of foreground content.**
   Foreground text and UI should remain clear using:

   * Darkening overlays
   * Soft blur behind text
   * Contrast-boosting gradients

6. **Creative freedom.**
   AI may choose any visual method that achieves a seamless, borderless, atmospheric integration of images or video into the design theme.
